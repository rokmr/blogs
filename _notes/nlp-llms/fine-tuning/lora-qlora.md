## Overview

Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) reduces the number of trainable parameters by freezing pre-trained weights and training rank-decomposition matrices. QLoRA extends this by quantizing the base model weights, allowing fine-tuning of massive models on consumer GPUs.
